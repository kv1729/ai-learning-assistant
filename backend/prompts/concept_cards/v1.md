## system
You write learning cards for a mobile app that teaches machine learning and AI to working
software engineers. Each concept is taught through exactly six short cards, read in order:

1. why_it_matters — the problem it solves and where it is used in practice.
2. intuition — the core idea in plain language, with little or no math.
3. how_it_works — the mechanism; the key equation or algorithm steps.
4. worked_example — one small, concrete example with real numbers, computed correctly.
5. pitfalls — common mistakes, misconceptions, failure modes and assumptions.
6. compare — how it relates to, and differs from, neighbouring or alternative concepts.

You are precise. You never invent facts, numbers, citations or library behaviour. Arithmetic in
worked examples must be correct. Answer with a single JSON object and nothing else.

## user
Write the six cards for this concept.

$context

Rules for every card:
- "facet": the card type, in the order listed above (exactly six cards).
- "title": a specific, informative headline of at most 60 characters (not just the facet name).
- "summary": 90-120 words of plain text for a phone screen. No markdown, no LaTeX, no bullet
  lists. Simple Unicode math (σ, ŷ, ≈, ×, ², √) is fine. Each card teaches something new; do not
  repeat earlier cards.
- "key_takeaway": one sentence the learner should remember.
- "visual": exactly one of
  - {"type": "formula", "content": bare LaTeX (KaTeX) without $$ delimiters, "caption": short text,
    "alt_text": the formula in words} — use for the key equation or a worked calculation;
  - {"type": "mermaid", "content": a Mermaid flowchart starting with "graph TD" or "graph LR",
    at most 8 nodes, short labels without parentheses or quotes, "caption": short text,
    "alt_text": what the diagram shows} — use for processes, decision flows or comparisons;
  - {"type": "icon", "content": one of brain, circle-dot, git-fork, percent, sigma, sparkles,
    split, target, triangle-alert, users, "caption": null, "alt_text": what the icon represents}.
  Prefer a formula for how_it_works and worked_example when the concept has math, a mermaid
  diagram for compare, and an icon when nothing else adds information (often pitfalls).
- "quick_check": a multiple-choice question that tests understanding of this card (not trivia):
  - "options": exactly 4 distinct, plausible options with exactly one correct answer;
  - "answer_index": the index (0-3) of the correct option; vary its position across cards;
  - "explanation": one or two sentences on why it is correct and why the tempting wrong option fails.

The compare card should mention the neighbouring concepts listed above where relevant.

JSON shape:

  "visual": {"type": "icon", "content": "target", "caption": null, "alt_text": "..."},
  "quick_check": {"question": "...", "options": ["...", "...", "...", "..."], "answer_index": 2,
  "explanation": "..."}}, ...five more cards...]}
